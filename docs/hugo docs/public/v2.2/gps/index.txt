# GPS &amp; Wardriving

> Overview of GhostESP&#39;s GPS and wardriving capabilities

---

GhostESP supports GPS modules for location tracking and wardriving. Capture wireless network data with GPS coordinates and upload to WiGLE for mapping and research.
On-device, the GPS menu provides GPS Info and BLE &#43; Wi-Fi Wardriving.
The GhostESP Companion app also supports [Phone GPS Wardriving](/v2.2/gps/companion-phone-wardriving/) - use your phone’s GPS to tag APs when your device doesn’t have a GPS module.
