## Hardware Connection
Connect your ESP32 device to your Flipper Zero via UART:
ESP32 Pin |
Flipper GPIO Pin |
TX |
GPIO 13 or 15 |
RX |
GPIO 14 or 16 |
GND |
GND |
VCC |
3.3V (if needed, check your board’s power requirements) |
Note: Pin configurations may vary depending on your Flipper Zero setup. Refer to your specific hardware documentation or the companion app’s connection guide.
## Software Connection
- Power on your GhostESP device
- Launch the GhostESP app on your Flipper Zero
- The app will automatically attempt to connect via serial
If the app can’t connect or the connection drops during use, see [Troubleshooting](/latest/flipper-app/troubleshooting/) for the full checklist.
